# Planned future upgrades

- **More onboard motor drivers**, including additional brushed DC, stepper and BLDC driver sections after individual channel validation.
- **More motor types and actuator diversity** without needing separate custom controllers.
- **Built-in sensors** such as an IMU, temperature or supply/current sensing where useful.
- **New expansion ports** with defined connector pinouts and power limits.
- **More communication interfaces** for radio, Wi-Fi/Bluetooth gateway, I2C, UART and other modules as appropriate.
- **Improved protections** such as reverse-polarity protection, fault indication, overcurrent feedback and safer treatment of regenerative loads.
- **Firmware library and examples** for motor modules, servos, sensors and relay/IO use.
- **Measured testing and documentation** comparing speed of assembly, faults and current/thermal limits.

These are goals, **not built-in features of R4**. First validate one controller and a tested module; then expand features based on measured results.

# Early two-channel relay prototype: Rev A

The included PDF documents a 6 Nov 2026 relay design with G2RL-1A-E relays, AO3400A transistor drivers, 1N4007 coil diodes, indicator LEDs and fuses. The concept uses a 12 V feed and targets two 10 A outputs, with real load tests still needed.

**Not the same as R4:** The newer carrier documentation lists Songle SRD-12VDC-SL-C relays and PN2222ABU drivers. Do not merge the old PCB BOM into the new board.
